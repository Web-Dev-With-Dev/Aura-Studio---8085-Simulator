Hardware Peripheral Visualizers

Real-time interactive port-driven hardware modules embedded inside the IDE.

7-Segment LED Display

Direct BCD/Hex decoding, raw bitmask layout (a-g, dp), and multiplexed 4-digit / 8-digit display drivers.

ADC/DAC Waveform Oscilloscope

Real-time digital storage oscilloscope telemetry for Sine, Sawtooth, Triangle & Square wave generation.

4-Way Traffic Light Controller

Simulates urban 4-phase road intersections with N-S and E-W signals plus pedestrian WALK indicators.

Stepper Motor Motion Simulator

Unipolar 4-coil stepper rotor motion visualizer supporting Full-Step CW/CCW and 8-step Half-Stepping microstepping.

16x2 Character LCD (HD44780)

Hitachi HD44780 controller emulator supporting full 8-bit command set and DDRAM character rendering.

8085 Trainer Kit Emulator

Full 8085 trainer kit keypad interface (RESET, HALT, SET/MEM, INR, DCR, GO, EXEC) mirroring physical hardware boards.
